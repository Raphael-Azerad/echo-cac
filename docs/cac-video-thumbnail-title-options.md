# Echo CAC Video Thumbnail And Title Options

Last reviewed: 2026-09-14

## Packaging Principle

Echo should be packaged as a human time-capsule app, not as a journal, notes app, productivity tool, or AI wrapper. The title and thumbnail should make judges understand the emotional premise quickly:

```text
Echo preserves a human, a place, and a time before the present becomes unreachable.
```

Use concrete language from the app: sealed, returned, archive, letter, voice, video, place, time, future self. Avoid startup phrasing like "reimagine memories," "AI-powered journaling," "the future of reflection," or "your personal memory platform."

## Recommended Pair

### Recommended Title

```text
Echo - Preserve a Human, a Place, and a Time
```

This is the strongest CAC-facing title because it is clear, distinctive, and emotionally exact. It tells judges what Echo does before they click, while avoiding the generic "time capsule app" phrasing that could make Echo sound like a notes app with a date picker.

### Recommended Thumbnail

Use or refine:

```text
docs/assets/video/echo-thumbnail-02-human-place-time.jpg
```

Thumbnail direction:

```text
A human. A place. A time.
Letters, voice, video, and context sealed for later.
```

Visual basis:

- Primary app frame: `docs/assets/submission/08-media-evidence-tray.jpg`
- Supporting frame: `docs/assets/submission/05-present-and-future-fields.jpg`
- Keep the warm ivory background, large serif type, bronze seal mark, and full product panels.
- Do not use the old fake phone crop. It made the app look cramped and lower quality.
- Keep the text sparse enough to read at small size. The three-part line is the hook; the subtitle is optional if the thumbnail feels crowded.

Why this pair works: the title names Echo's thesis, and the thumbnail proves it visually with media, place, and returned archive context. It says "this captures a whole moment," not "this stores notes."

## Title Options

1. `Echo - Preserve a Human, a Place, and a Time`
   Best overall. Specific, memorable, and faithful to the product.

2. `Echo - A Private Time Capsule for a Future Self`
   Clearest plain-English option. Good if the upload needs to be instantly understandable.

3. `Echo - Notes Save Words. Echo Saves the Moment.`
   Strong problem/solution title. Best if the video opens by contrasting notes with richer memory.

4. `Echo - A Letter That Waits Until You Are Ready`
   Warm and literary. Best if the edit leans into the returned Echo reveal.

5. `Echo - Sealed Letters for the Person You Become`
   Human and slightly poetic, while still saying what the app does.

6. `Echo - Before This Moment Disappears`
   More emotional and cinematic. Best for a slower opening with voiceover.

7. `Echo - The App That Keeps Future Letters Sealed`
   Practical and privacy-forward. Strong if judges need to understand the core rule quickly.

8. `Echo - Private Memories That Return Later`
   Accessible, simple, and less technical. Slightly more generic than the top choices.

9. `Echo - A Quiet Archive for Returned Selves`
   Most literary. Best if the thumbnail clearly shows the archive UI.

10. `Echo - Write Now. Return Later.`
    Short and clean. Good for a thumbnail pairing, but too minimal if used alone.

11. `Echo - Saving the Room Around a Memory`
    Distinctive and warm. Best if the opening mentions voice, place, weather, and media.

12. `Echo - A Local-First Time Capsule App`
    Most technical. Use only if the submission context needs local-first privacy in the title.

## Opening Hook Scripts

### Hook 1: Best Overall

```text
Most apps save files. Echo saves the moment around a person: what they wrote, what they sounded like, where they were, and what they wanted a future version of themselves to remember.
```

### Hook 2: More Literary

```text
I built Echo for the version of a person that usually disappears. Before the polished story, before the outcome, there is a room, a voice, a place, and a letter that can wait.
```

### Hook 3: More Technical And Judge-Friendly

```text
Echo is a local-first time capsule app. A user can write, record voice or video, capture place and weather, and seal everything until a future date, while the app hides private content until it returns.
```

## Final Direction

Use this unless the final video changes substantially:

```text
Title: Echo - Preserve a Human, a Place, and a Time
Thumbnail: A human. A place. A time.
Opening line: Most apps save files. Echo saves the moment around a person.
```

This direction is warm enough to feel human, concrete enough for judges, and specific enough to keep Echo out of the "journal app" category.

## Editing Software Recommendation

### Best overall: DaVinci Resolve

Use DaVinci Resolve for the main edit if you are willing to spend a little time learning it. It has a free version and official support for editing, color correction, visual effects, motion graphics, and audio post-production in one app.

Source: https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve

Best for:

- Main timeline edit.
- Clean screen-recording pacing.
- Audio balancing for Raphael's narration.
- Simple title cards.
- Exporting a polished 1080p or 4K final video.

### Fastest caption helper: CapCut

Use CapCut if captions become annoying to do manually. CapCut's site highlights AI captions/auto-caption workflows, which can be helpful after the main video is edited.

Source: https://www.capcut.com/

Best for:

- Auto captions.
- Quick caption timing fixes.
- A captioned export if the upload platform does not provide good captions.

Do not use CapCut's loud social-video style for Echo. Avoid meme fonts, sticker effects, bounce transitions, and fast cuts.

### Best paid Mac option: Final Cut Pro

Use Final Cut Pro if you already have it or strongly prefer Apple's editing interface. Apple's page presents it as pro-quality editing for Mac and iPad and offers a free trial.

Source: https://www.apple.com/final-cut-pro/

Best for:

- Fast Mac-native editing.
- Clean timeline workflow.
- Polished exports without learning DaVinci.

### Best if already subscribed: Adobe Premiere Pro

Use Premiere Pro only if you already have Adobe access or specifically want the Adobe workflow. Adobe lists Premiere as part of Creative Cloud and offers student/teacher pricing.

Source: https://www.adobe.com/products/premiere.html

Best for:

- Professional timeline editing if you already know Adobe.
- Captioning and audio cleanup inside one ecosystem.

For Echo, Premiere is not necessary. DaVinci Resolve plus optional CapCut captions is the most practical setup.
