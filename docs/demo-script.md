# Echo CAC Demo Script

## Positioning

Echo captures a human, a place, and a time before the present self becomes hard to reach. It is not another journal, notes app, or AI wrapper: it is a local-first time capsule where someone can write, speak, record, attach media, preserve moment context, and seal the artifact for a future date or future version of themselves.

## Two-Minute Recording Path

1. Start on the walkthrough screen and say: "Most apps preserve files. Echo preserves the interior context around a person: what they sounded like, where they were, what they hoped for, and what they needed their future self to remember."
2. Tap the live walkthrough. Show that returned Echoes appear in the archive, while sealed Echoes are counted without exposing their titles, letters, media names, mood, location, weather, or goals.
3. Open Write. Show the letter room, voice note, video note, dictation, photo/file attachments, selected-text polish, and Moment capture. Emphasize that freewriting remains the center and the optional polish never reads the full letter.
4. Open the Trust layer. Say: "The technical architecture matches the product promise: local-first IndexedDB storage, Web Speech and MediaRecorder capture, a Docker-ready context API, Promptfoo policy coverage, and automated tests for sealed-content privacy."
5. Open a returned Echo. Let the reveal screen land first, then open the artifact and show the letter, media, place, weather, and facts together as a quiet archive object.

## Truthful Buzzwords

- Local-first privacy architecture
- Sealed-content access control
- IndexedDB persistence layer
- Web Speech API dictation
- MediaRecorder voice and video capture
- Docker-ready context API
- API boundary that never receives letter text
- Promptfoo AI policy gate
- Playwright accessibility and privacy regression suite
- Human-centered archival UX

## Guardrails To Say Out Loud

Echo was built with AI development tools, but the product is not an AI chatbot. The current app only includes a small selected-text polish affordance, and the most private parts of an Echo stay on the user's device unless the user exports them.
